ClearSignal

See your real Shopify conversion rate without bot traffic

ClearSignal is a Shopify app for merchants whose sessions look inflated by bots and whose conversion rate looks too low. It sorts your store's sessions into real shoppers, good AI agents, bad bots and unclassified traffic, using its own detection as a second opinion next to Shopify's. It shows your conversion rate with bad bot and AI agent sessions taken out, estimates the ad spend that goes to bots, and shows where bot traffic comes from by country and data center. It measures rather than blocks, and it doesn't change Shopify's own reports.

What ClearSignal shows you

  • Who is visiting. Every session sorted into human, good agent, bad bot or unclassified.
  • Your true conversion rate. Your conversion rate with bad bot and AI agent sessions taken out of the count.
  • What bots cost you. An estimate of the ad spend that went to bot visits, once you enter your monthly ad spend.
  • AI agent traffic. The AI assistants and crawlers visiting your store, kept in their own group instead of being counted as bad bots.
  • Where bots come from. Bot traffic by country, data center provider and traffic channel, so you can see which sources it hits.
  • Answers in your admin. Ask Sidekick for your bot rate without opening the app.
  • Report views that fit your store. Leave countries you don't sell to out of your report views.

How it works

  1. Install and turn on the app embed

    ClearSignal adds an app embed to your theme and a web pixel. It counts traffic from the day you turn on the app embed.

  2. ClearSignal sorts each session

    The embed sends signals such as how the visitor moves and scrolls, the referrer and the landing page, and ClearSignal classifies the session. IP addresses are shortened before anything is stored.

  3. Read the numbers and decide

    Your dashboard shows your true conversion rate, what bots cost you and the AI agents visiting your store. You decide what to change, such as ad targeting.

Who it's for

Shopify merchants whose sessions jumped without more orders, who run ads and want to know how much of the traffic they pay for is bots, or who want to see how many shoppers now arrive through AI agents.

Common questions

Does ClearSignal block bots?

No. It measures and reports; it doesn't block visitors. Leaving a country out of your reports only changes what your reports show.

Why is my Shopify store getting traffic from Singapore or China?

Often it's bots running on cloud servers in data centers, which show up as visitors from that country. ClearSignal breaks bot traffic down by country and data center provider so you can see how much is automated. It doesn't block any of it.

Does Shopify already filter bot traffic out of analytics?

Partly. Shopify filters the bot sessions it identifies out of its session reports by default, but some automated traffic gets through and older sessions aren't reclassified. ClearSignal shows its own view next to Shopify's as a second opinion and doesn't change Shopify's reports.

Why does my Shopify conversion rate look wrong?

Bots add sessions that never buy, which lowers your conversion rate even when orders stay the same. ClearSignal shows your conversion rate with bad bot and AI agent sessions taken out. It counts from the day you turn on its app embed, so it can't correct earlier weeks.

Does ClearSignal track AI agent traffic?

Yes. It sorts identified AI assistants and crawlers into their own good agent group, separate from bad bots, and leaves them out of your true conversion rate.

What data does ClearSignal collect?

Session signals such as mouse and scroll activity, screen size, the referrer and the landing page, plus a session ID. IP addresses are shortened before they are stored or logged, and bot detection, the visit cookie and the web pixel run only when the shopper allows analytics. The privacy policy lists everything.

Will it slow my store?

The app embed's script is deferred, and its bot detection loads when the browser is idle, so neither holds up your page while it loads.
