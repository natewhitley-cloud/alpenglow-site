IntakePilot

Turn B2B purchase orders into Shopify draft orders

IntakePilot is a Shopify app for wholesale order entry. Upload or paste a buyer's PO, or forward the email it came in. IntakePilot reads every line, matches the buyer and each product to your store, and writes a draft order with the PO number, terms and ship-to. Each line shows the PO text it came from, anything that needs a second look is flagged, and nothing becomes an order until you approve it.

What IntakePilot does

  • Reads any PO. PDFs and scans, spreadsheets, CSV files, photos and email text.
  • Matches to your store. Finds the buyer and matches each line to your products by SKU, barcode or description.
  • Shows its work. Every draft line sits next to the PO text it came from.
  • Flags what to check. Quantity, unit, duplicate and revised-PO issues, line by line.
  • You approve every order. Drafts stay drafts until a member of your staff approves them.

How it works

  1. Send in the PO

    Upload the file, paste the order text, or forward the buyer's email to your store's own intake address.

  2. IntakePilot reads and matches

    It reads every line, finds the buyer and the products in your store, and writes a Shopify draft order from your catalog.

  3. Check and approve

    Fix anything flagged, then approve. The draft becomes an order in Shopify, and the app keeps count of how many went through as read.

Who it's for

Shopify merchants who sell wholesale and still retype purchase orders that buyers send as PDFs, spreadsheets or emails. It isn't for stores that want orders placed with no one checking them.

Common questions

Does IntakePilot place orders on its own?

No. It writes a draft order and flags anything to check. A member of your staff approves each draft before it becomes an order.

What kinds of POs can it read?

PDFs (including scans), spreadsheets, CSV files, photos of a printed PO, and order text pasted into the app or sent in an email.

What happens when it can't match something?

It doesn't guess. A buyer it can't match asks you to choose one, and a line it can't match to a product is flagged so you can pick the right one before you approve.

Does it catch duplicate or revised POs?

Yes. A PO that was already entered is flagged instead of becoming a second order, and a revised PO shows what changed so you can apply or keep it.

Do I need Shopify B2B?

No. It works with B2B companies and with regular customers, so it fits wholesale buyers however your store is set up.

Is there a free plan?

Free and paid plans available. See the IntakePilot listing on the Shopify App Store for features and plans.
