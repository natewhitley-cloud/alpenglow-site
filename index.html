Your back office, handled.

Small-business tools for Shopify and monday.com that do the tedious work, filter out the noise, and flag what's costing you money. You make the final call.

Save time

Does the tedious work for you.

  • PostroomIQ

    Turns forwarded emails and their attachments into monday.com board items, and sends anything uncertain to you to review.

  • DedupeIQ

    Finds duplicate items on your monday.com boards and merges them after you review.

Filter the noise

Separates what matters from what doesn't.

  • ClearSignal

    Separates real shoppers from bad bots and AI agents, so you see your true conversion rate.

  • FraudPilot

    Puts the orders Shopify flags as risky in one queue with the reasons, so you can decide each one quickly.

Catch costly mistakes

Shows you what's wrong and where, so you decide what to change.

  • TaxDelta

    Finds sales tax discrepancies in refunds and undercollected orders, so you can check them before you file.

  • GhostCode

    Finds code that uninstalled apps left in your theme, down to the file and line. It never edits your theme.
