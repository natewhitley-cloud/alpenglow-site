Plans differ only in how many items you get each month.

What counts as an item

  • An item is one email that AI turns into a board item. It covers up to 5 attachment pages.
  • Each extra 5 pages counts as one more item.
  • These are free: emails marked Filtered (not relevant), duplicates, and failures.
  • Your count resets on the 1st of each month (UTC).

Over the limit

  • No email is lost. It still lands on your board as an item marked "Over limit", without AI reading it.
  • You can process it once you upgrade or your limit resets.

Free plan

  • Free reads the same files as paid plans: email text, PDFs (including scans), photos, faxes, Excel, Word, CSV and forwarded emails.
  • On paid plans and trials, harder emails (photos, scans, values the first read wasn't sure of) get a second read by a larger AI model. On Free, the larger model only double-checks an email before it's filtered out (up to 3 a month).

Trials and billing

  • Start with a 14-day free trial of Pro.
  • Subscriptions, upgrades, downgrades and cancellations are handled in monday.com's plan selection. Yearly plans cost 10 times the monthly price.
  • Billing and refunds go through monday.com. You can get a full refund within 30 days, processed by monday.com.
  • Plans are per monday account, with no per-seat pricing.
