Cases vs units on Shopify wholesale orders: stop "4 cases" shipping as 4 units

Updated October 28, 2026

Short answer: decide what one unit means in Shopify, then make it impossible to miss. Either sell the case as its own variant (Case of 12, with its own SKU and price), so a PO for 4 cases is a quantity of 4, or sell single units with a B2B quantity rule that only allows multiples of 12, so 4 cases is 48. Put the pack size in the product or variant name either way (for example "sold in cases of 12"), and when you enter a PO, write the conversion down instead of doing it in your head.

Why this goes wrong

Buyers order in whatever unit they think in: "4 cs", "2 dozen", "1 master carton", "48 ea". Shopify's quantity field has no unit; it's a count of whatever the variant is. When the person entering the PO and the person who set up the product mean different things by one, the order ships 4 jars instead of 4 cases, or 48 cases instead of 48 jars. Both are expensive, and both look fine on screen.

Quantity rules and volume pricing are B2B catalog features, available on the Basic, Grow and Advanced plans as well as Shopify Plus. Increments apply to each variant: a buyer can't mix 6 of one flavor and 6 of another to make a case of 12. If your cases are mixed, a case variant (or a separate product for the mixed case) is the cleaner choice.

Setting up each option

A case variant

  1. On the product, add an option such as Pack with values Single and Case of 12 (or add the case as its own product if it has its own barcode and listing).
  2. Give the case a SKU that says what it is, such as HONEY-12OZ-CS12, and its own price.
  3. Set its inventory in cases. If singles and cases come from the same stock, decide who moves stock between them, and when.

A quantity increment

  1. Go to Markets > Catalogs, open the wholesale catalog, then Products and pricing > Manage > Manage products and pricing.
  2. For each variant sold by the case, set the increment to the case size, and a minimum if you have one.
  3. If you offer volume pricing, set each break at a multiple of the increment (24, 48, 96).
  4. Make sure your theme shows the minimum on the product page, so buyers ordering online know how many to add.

Entering a PO without quantity mistakes

  1. Read the unit on every line, not just the number. Look for cs, case, ctn, carton, dz, dozen, pk, pack, ea, each, unit.
  2. Convert to the unit your product uses. With a case variant, 4 cases is 4. With singles, 4 cases of 12 is 48, and 2 dozen is 24.
  3. Watch for nested packs. "1 master carton" might be 4 inner packs of 6. Confirm with the buyer the first time and write it on their account.
  4. Write the conversion in the draft's note for any line you converted, such as "Line 3: PO says 4 cs, entered 48 ea". Whoever checks or picks the order can see what you did.
  5. Check against the increment. If you use increments, a quantity that isn't a multiple of the case size is a sign the conversion went wrong. Quantity rules are checked when buyers order online; don't rely on them to catch a quantity you type into a draft yourself.
  6. Ask when the unit is missing. "Honey x 4" from a buyer who usually orders cases is a question to send, not a guess to make.

Common questions

How do I sell by the case on Shopify wholesale?

Two common ways. Add the case as its own variant, such as Case of 12, with its own SKU and price, so one case is a quantity of 1. Or keep selling single units and set a B2B quantity rule on the catalog with an increment of 12, so buyers can only order in multiples of 12.

Do quantity increments apply across variants?

No. Shopify's increment rules apply to each product variant, so a buyer can't combine 5 gray hats and 5 blue hats to meet an increment of 10 hats.

Are B2B quantity rules available without Shopify Plus?

Yes. Shopify lists quantity rules (minimum, maximum and increments) and volume pricing for the Basic, Grow and Advanced plans as well as Shopify Plus.

Does a case variant share inventory with the single units?

No. Each variant has its own inventory count, so selling a case variant doesn't reduce the count of single units. If you sell both, you need to move stock between them yourself or use an app that links them.
