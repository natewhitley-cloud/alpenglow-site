Support

Support for every Alpenglow app is by email: support@alpenglowsoftware.com

What to include

So we can look into it without a back-and-forth, tell us:

  • Which app you're writing about.
  • Your store or account URL: your Shopify store address (for example yourstore.myshopify.com) or your monday.com account address (for example yourteam.monday.com).
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen.
  • Screenshots, if you have them.

Please don't send passwords, API keys or payment card details by email.
